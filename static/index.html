Quantification Methodology

Transparent mathematical formula explaining calculated returns.
Initiative Formula
Annual Value = Addressable Base × Improvement × Adoption × Confidence
  • Addressable base: Segment of the cost or revenue pool targeted.
  • Improvement: Projected optimization rate based on benchmarks.
  • Adoption: Rollout rate planned for the phase.
  • Confidence: Adjustment factor derived from evidence and data maturity.
